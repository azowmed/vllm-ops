    Model. Each engine step runs two kernel classes one after the other. Dense GEMMs take max(FLOPs ÷ compute, weight bytes ÷ bandwidth). Attention takes max(FLOPs ÷ compute, KV bytes ÷ bandwidth). The step adds the two classes and, with tensor parallelism, two all-reduces per layer. MoE layers read only the experts their tokens hit, assuming uniform routing.

    Not modeled: scheduler and CPU overhead, sampling, preemption recompute, prefix-cache hits, speculative decoding, power and clock throttling, pipeline parallelism. Expect measurements below these numbers, not above.

    Specs: NVIDIA datasheets, dense tensor TFLOPS. RTX PRO 6000, B300 and Rubin are approximate or announced.
